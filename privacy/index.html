Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy covers roesink.dev and the projects I publish under it. They are personal projects by an independent software developer. Projects that process personal data beyond what is described here have their own policy, listed below, which adds to this one.

Who is responsible

Sander Roesink, Hengelo, the Netherlands, is the data controller. For any privacy question or request, email sander@roesink.dev.

Principles

  • I only collect personal data a project needs to work.
  • I do not sell personal data, and I do not use it for advertising.
  • I do not use analytics or tracking cookies, unless a project's own policy says otherwise.
  • Where a project stores personal data, it is kept only as long as the project needs it, and you can ask me to delete it.

This website

roesink.dev is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. Both process your IP address to deliver and protect the traffic. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser fetches them.

The site sets no cookies. Local storage in your browser only remembers your chosen color theme.

Project policies

  • Meander: running and walking route planner, including the Garmin Connect integration.

Legal basis

Personal data is processed to provide the service you ask for (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), and to keep websites and services secure and working (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Email sander@roesink.dev to use these rights. You can also lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
