Meander Privacy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy explains what personal data Meander processes. Meander is a running and walking route planner available at meander.roesink.dev and meander.sander.ninja. It is a personal, non-commercial project. The general privacy policy also applies.

Who is responsible

Sander Roesink, Hengelo, the Netherlands, is the data controller. For any privacy question or request, email sander@roesink.dev.

Route planning

To plan a route, Meander uses the start and end locations, the distance and the route preferences you enter, plus any place names you search for. Routing, place search and map data all run on servers I host myself. Your locations are not sent to outside routing or map providers.

Routes are saved so the link to them keeps working. A saved route contains the route and the request it came from. It does not include your name or any account details. Anyone with the link can open it, so share a route link only with people you want to see it.

No accounts, analytics or advertising

Meander has no user accounts. It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and it does not sell or share your data.

Connected services (Garmin Connect)

You can choose to connect Meander to your Garmin Connect account, so you can send a planned route to your Garmin device as a course. When you connect:

  • Garmin asks you to approve the connection on its own website. Meander never sees your Garmin password.
  • Meander receives an access token, a refresh token and your Garmin user ID. These are stored encrypted. They are linked only to an anonymous session cookie in your browser, not to your name or email address.
  • Meander uses the connection only to send a course when you explicitly ask it to. A course contains the route's name, path, distance, elevation and activity type (running or walking).
  • Meander does not read your activities, health data or any other data from your Garmin account.

You can disconnect at any time in Meander, or by removing Meander from the connected apps in your Garmin Connect account. In both cases Meander deletes the stored tokens and your Garmin user ID right away. Courses you already sent stay in Garmin Connect until you delete them there. Garmin's own handling of your data is described in the Garmin privacy policy.

Who processes Garmin data

  • The Garmin data Meander holds (the tokens and your Garmin user ID) is stored and processed only on my own server in the Netherlands. Meander talks to Garmin directly from that server.
  • Cloudflare, the reverse proxy in front of Meander, carries the encrypted traffic between your browser and Meander, including the return from Garmin's consent screen. It does not store or use Garmin data.
  • No other hosting provider, integration partner, technology vendor or data processing service receives Garmin data.
  • Garmin data is never sold, and it is not shared with or disclosed to any third party.
  • No AI or machine learning system processes Garmin data, and Garmin data is never used to train, evaluate or improve AI models.

Your Garmin data and your choices

  • Withdraw consent and delete: click Disconnect in Meander, or remove Meander in Garmin Connect. Meander then revokes its access at Garmin where possible and deletes the stored tokens and Garmin user ID.
  • Access, export or correct: email sander@roesink.dev to receive a copy of the Garmin-related data Meander holds about your connection, or to have it corrected or deleted. Include the Garmin user ID or the approximate time you connected, so I can find it.

Cookies and local storage

  • meander_sid: a random, anonymous identifier that links your browser to its connected services. It is set only when you connect a service and expires after one year.
  • meander_oauth: a temporary, encrypted cookie that protects the connection flow. It is deleted after the flow ends or after 10 minutes.
  • Local storage keeps your theme, language and whether you have seen the editing tip. This information stays in your browser.

These are all strictly necessary for the features you use, so no consent banner is shown.

Hosting and technical data

Meander runs on my own server in the Netherlands and is served through Cloudflare, which processes your IP address to deliver and protect the traffic.

Meander keeps your IP address in memory for a short time to apply rate limits. It is not stored. Server logs contain technical details such as errors and request paths. They are used only to operate and debug the service.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser fetches them.

Legal basis and retention

Personal data is processed to provide the service you ask for (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), and to keep the service secure and working (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Tokens for connected services are kept until you disconnect. Saved routes are kept so their links keep working. You can ask me to delete a route at any time.

Your rights

Your rights, and how to use them, are described in the general privacy policy.
